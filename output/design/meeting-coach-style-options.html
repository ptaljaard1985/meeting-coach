Put it into practice

Five things to take forward

  1. 01

    Lead with the person

    Make space for life before looking at figures. Reflect what the client says so the opening shapes the conversation.

    In this meeting The adviser opened with a check-in and reflected Client A’s wish for more control over time (segments 1–3).

  2. 02

    Let each voice shape the agenda

    Invite each client to say what would make the meeting useful. Use those answers to agree the order together.

    In this meeting Client B was asked what would make the conversation useful, and the adviser placed that concern first (segments 5–7).

  3. 03

    Name the feeling first

    Reflect the worry before moving into analysis. Check the reflection so the client can correct your understanding.

    In this meeting The adviser reflected that time mattered as much as money and asked whether that was understood correctly (segment 3).

  4. 04

    Make next steps concrete

    Give each action an owner and a time. Ask about obstacles while there is still room to work through them.

    In this meeting Both clients were asked to send work-hour estimates by Friday, followed by a question about what could get in the way (segment 10).

  5. 05

    Close with a shared readback

    Restate the commitments before ending. Allow each client to confirm or correct the account in their own voice.

    In this meeting The adviser read back the plan and both clients confirmed the agreed steps (segments 13–15).
